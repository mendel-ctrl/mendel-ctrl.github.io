About FLAME

FLAME 2026

Chabad Young Professionals S. Pete's yearly Chanukah party for Jewish 20s & 30s, presented by the Chabad Jewish Center of Greater S. Petersburg.

This year FLAME lights up the outdoor rooftop space at the brand‑new Chabad. Light the menorah under the stars with the best crowd in S. Pete, while the city glows all around you.

WhenSat, Dec. 5th · 8 PM
WhereRooftop at the new Chabad · 533 4th St N
